# Privacy Policy — Word Rain

**Last updated: October 10, 2026**

This policy describes the data processed by the **Word Rain** mobile game
(Android application ID: `com.lestieve.wordrain`), published by **Lestieve**.

## Contact

For questions about this policy or personal data, contact the developer through
the application's page on the Google Play Store.

## Age of players

Word Rain is intended for people aged **13 and over**. The game is not designed
for children under 13 and does not ask them to provide personal data. If you
believe that a child under 13 has provided us with data, contact the developer
through the application's Google Play page.

## Data processed by the game

The game does not require an account and, in its current version, does not
operate its own server to which gameplay or player profiles are sent. The
following data is stored in the application's storage on the device:

- settings (language, dictionary, sound, vibration, and gameplay options);
- scores and game history;
- favorite words and, where applicable, associated domains;
- custom game modes created by the player;
- local counters and indicators related to games, ads, and Premium access.

This data is used to remember the player's choices, display their history, and
provide the game's features. Names and domains entered in favorites or custom
modes remain on the device in the application's current operation.

## Third-party services

Some features use services provided by third parties. Those providers may
process technical data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

### Advertising — Google AdMob

Word Rain uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads to players
who do not have Premium access. The published version uses Word Rain's own
AdMob application and ad units, in three formats: banner, interstitial, and
rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are shown only if the player chooses to watch them.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google may collect and process data such
as the device's advertising identifier (for example, the Android Advertising
ID), IP address, information about the device and application (model,
operating system version, language), and interactions with ads (impressions,
clicks). This data may be used to serve and measure ads, which may be
personalized, and to prevent fraud and abuse. The exact data depends on the
device, region, and your settings. Lestieve does not receive this data directly
and does not use it for its own purposes.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in your
device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads). Where required by
applicable law, such as in the EEA, consent for personalized ads may be
requested and can be managed or withdrawn. Players with Premium access do not
see ads. For more information about Google's processing, see the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Google's information about advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### In-app purchases — Google Play

Purchases and restoration of Premium access are handled through the device's
app store billing service, including Google Play on Android. The store
processes the information necessary to handle payment and manage the purchase.
The application receives the purchase status and uses it to activate or restore
Premium; Lestieve does not receive payment details through the game.

### Google Fonts

The application uses the Google Fonts library. If font files are not already
available in the device's cache, downloading them may contact Google's services
and disclose technical connection information such as the IP address.

### Links and reviews

The game may open the Wiktionary website or the application's store listing,
for example, to view a source or leave a review. These services are opened
outside the game and are governed by their own policies. The game does not
receive the content of a review submitted in the store.

## Retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on the device until it is deleted using management
features available in the game, the application's data is cleared in the
device settings, or the game is uninstalled. Availability after a restore may
also depend on the device's and platform's backup features.

Data processed by Google or the store is subject to their own retention periods
and management tools. Refer to their policies and settings to manage it.

## Security

The game data described above is stored in the application's space on the
device. Protect access to your device and use the platform's backup settings
according to your preferences. Transmissions and processing performed by
third-party services are also subject to those providers' security measures.

## Your choices and rights

You can delete local data by clearing Word Rain's data in the device settings
or uninstalling the application. For data held by Google or the store, use
their privacy and account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase,
restrict, or object to the processing of personal data. To submit a request to
Lestieve, use the application's page on Google Play. Requests concerning data
processed directly by Google or the store should be sent to those providers.

## GDPR — European Economic Area

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR) may give you rights over your personal data. These may
include the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of
processing, or portability, and the right to object where applicable. You may
also withdraw consent where a processing activity is based on your consent;
withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out
before withdrawal.

For data processed by Lestieve in connection with the game, contact the
developer through the application's page on Google Play to exercise these
rights or ask questions about the legal basis for processing. You also have
the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory
authority. Where Google or another provider processes data under its own
responsibility, you may need to contact that provider directly to exercise
your rights.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or the third-party services
it uses change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last
updated.
